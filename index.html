Meet the artist

Ambreen, known to her brides as Pinky.

Pinky is a qualified beautician and make-up artist with over two decades in the beauty industry. She has become one of the best-known names in the Greater Toronto Area for her bridal make-up and her intricate mehendi.

She specializes in Pakistani bridal looks and is just as fluent in Indian bridal traditions. She starts by listening. She learns your outfit, your jewellery, your skin and the story of your day. Then she creates a look that still feels like you, only more radiant. Her brides say they don't just look beautiful. They feel calm, cared for and completely themselves.
